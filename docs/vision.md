# Vision

Daily Goals is the first app of **Actualize**, a suite for self-actualization: become your ideal. On the App Store it is listed as "Actualize: Daily Goals"; in hand it stays Daily Goals. The feature list lives on the roadmap, [Issue #40](https://github.com/patrick-k-oneill/daily-goals/issues/40).

## Purpose

Strengthen the person, then their health, then uncover the potential a learning curve had put out of reach: an instrument, painting, organizing. The tools and the knowledge already exist. The app removes the barrier to using them by making entry and habit-forming fun, rewarding, and above all easy. Goals flex with feedback and results instead of staying fixed.

## Principles

- Meet the user where they are, whether well on their way or at rock bottom, with compassion.
- Never reinforce or enable harmful behavior, and say so from a patient, curious place.
- Minimal surface: Daily Goals, Calendar, Journal.
- Frontier models come from subscriptions the user already pays for (handoff) and from on-device models. No keys, no backend.
- Built for two people first. No expectations beyond that, until someone else's product does it better for less.
